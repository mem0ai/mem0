With BYOK, every LLM call that writes your memories runs on a model you choose, billed to your key. On [LoCoMo](https://github.com/snap-research/locomo), a public benchmark of 10 long conversations and 1,540 questions about them, we tested the most used and most capable models that work with BYOK, open-weight and closed. The results below show how accurate each model's memories are, what they cost and how long an add takes.

## Set up BYOK

1. As an organization owner, open **Settings → BYOK** in the [Mem0 dashboard](https://app.mem0.ai/dashboard/settings?tab=org-llm).
2. Pick a provider: OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter or Azure OpenAI.
3. Paste your API key and enter the model. On OpenRouter, use the model's full ID, such as `xiaomi/mimo-v2.6-flash`. Azure OpenAI also needs your endpoint URL and deployment name.
4. Click **Save provider**. Mem0 writes your memories with that model from then on. **Disable provider** switches back to Mem0's default model.

## How we tested

- Every model took the same test, and only the model changed: it makes every LLM call in an add.
- Accuracy is the share of LoCoMo's questions answered correctly from each model's memories, graded against LoCoMo's reference answers and averaged over two runs.
- Cost is estimated from each model's calls at list prices, per 1,000 LoCoMo adds (about 8 messages each).
- Latency is the p95 time from an add call until its memories can be found: 19 of every 20 adds finish within that time. Adds slowed by a provider's rate limit are left out: that wait depends on your provider and plan. Results from October 2026.
